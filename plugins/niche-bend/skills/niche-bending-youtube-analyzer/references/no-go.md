# No-go list

Formats and niches that are out for us, with the reason. Read before a research round so they are dropped at the door. Add to it after every keep round: when Michael leaves a whole family unkept or says something is no good, write it here in his words with the date.

An entry here rules out the **channel as a model**. A single gene can still be taken from a no-go channel (a title formula from a presenter channel, a thumbnail layout from a footage channel) when it passes the outlier test.
