Scout across subject niches for fresh title/thumbnail formulas, narrated 2D
animation, whiteboard, maps, diagrams, cutouts, collage and animated typography.
Use actual video language rather than translated search titles. Verify exact
view counts from video/channel pages or feeds; localized K/M units can mislead.

Handoff the live Gallery URL, never an unlabelled localhost development preview.

Emerging channel proof requires under 100K subscribers, a median of at least 50K
across recent long-form videos, repeated hits (best/median at most 10x or the
three latest videos over 100K), first VIDEO under six months, last upload within
14 days, best at least 5x subscriber count, faceless execution and makeability.
Account join date is not first-video date. Unknown checks stay unknown. A recent
outlier at least 5x its channel median or subscribers may supply a packaging gene
even when the channel fails; mark it Format only. Established creators prove
topic demand, not an emerging opportunity. No-go sources are not channel models.
Packaging sources are at most 30 days old; craft references at most six months.
Compare the channel's top three fitting recent sources, with real media.

Start from other-niche packaging. Preserve the source title formula, recognized
nouns, credible human conflict and a visible contradiction. Keep the original
title beside each proposed title; bracket unchecked facts. Separate target
demand from saturation at the exact topic/format crossing, using comparable
month/year searches with history/explained wording and real evidence thumbnails.
Read relevance manually. Failed low-view copies are not successful competition;
an empty crossing is not proof of success. Check a separate outside-YouTube
signal (Wikipedia pageviews or Google Trends). Never blend these into a made-up
score. Video language is not verified audience geography. Public view counts do
not establish CTR or retention; those remain unknown without creator analytics.

Propose target NICHES plus concrete TITLES, not only promising source channels.
Define the target content job, actual language and exact topic + framing/title
formula + format crossing. Show the original title and thumbnail beside ours,
with the gene lineage and one-variable change. Independently verify every
transfer through three scouts: topic demand/language/urgency, actual target
audience visual/structural fit, and exact-crossing competition/counterexamples.
They investigate separately without copying each other's verdict. Then a fourth
independent critical reconciler reviews raw evidence, competing explanations
and the strongest reason the idea fails; this reviewer is not the proposer.
Use sequential waves when needed. If this cloud context lacks independent-agent
capability, disclose the failed gate and keep candidates Untested, not validated.

Inspect 8–12 unique on-topic target long-form videos across 3–5 channels. Show
each surveyed channel's top three fitting recent videos with original title,
thumbnail, checked views/date and a chronological frame sequence; these strips
can overlap the analytical sample or include extra context. Never pad missing
coverage. Big/presenter channels can show demand, conventions and competition,
but cannot prove an emerging small-channel opportunity or our faceless model.
Fitness → women-focused yoga must inspect actual yoga channels, including
whether viewers need follow-along movement or explanatory diagrams. Do not
assume the transfer or infer gender/age/country from views or thumbnails.

missing/negative evidence into a green recommendation. Keep source validation,
target demand, exact competition and target-fit decision separate.
