# Format families

Plain-word names for the looks we keep meeting, with channels that use them. Use these names on boards so Michael learns the vocabulary. The channels listed were checked against three in-video frames per video on 4 Oct 2026, except the two "No" rows at the bottom, which are from thumbnails and Danilov's comments. Add a family when a new one shows up.

A format has three parts. Name all three when you describe a channel:
- **The look**: what is on screen.
- **The structure**: how a video is built (a list, one question, a timeline, a tour).
- **The title formula**: the sentence with a gap that every upload reuses.
